Direct SMTP with the plain account password does not work, verified empirically rather than assumed:
- smtp.gmail.com:465 (SMTP_SSL) -> 535-5.7.8 Username and Password not accepted... https://support.google.com/mail/?p=BadCredentials
- smtp.gmail.com:587 (STARTTLS) -> same 535/BadCredentials error
