Pick a packing

Fit n unit squares inside one big square. Tilting is allowed — and for most counts it's the only way. The number under each count is the smallest square anyone has ever managed; blue means the record needs tilted squares.

Your board is that record plus a little slack, added down the right side and along the bottom — so the top-left corner is always the honest one. All the squares start on the sheet at half size; double-click one to grow it.

The idea

You start with every square already on the sheet at half size, so nothing is in a queue and nothing is in your way. Grow them one at a time and slide them home. Stuck? Shrink a neighbour back down and the space it was holding is yours again.

What counts as solved

Every square inside the border, no two overlapping. A whisker of overlap is forgiven so you never lose to a rounding error — a fortieth of the slack you were given, at most a five-hundredth of a square's edge. Tight boards have to be solved honestly.
